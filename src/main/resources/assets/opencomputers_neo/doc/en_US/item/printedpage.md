# Printed Page

![A physical copy of digital text.](item:opencomputers:printed_page)

Printed Pages are produced by the [Document Printer](../block/documentprinter.md).

A page stores its title, formatted lines, document/copy information, and identifiers used by the printer. Right-click a page to read it. Pages can also be placed in the printer's scanner slot and recovered with the `scan()` callback.

Printed Pages can be stored in [Folders](folder.md) and [File Cabinets](../block/filecabinet.md), or recycled with the [Shredder](../block/shredder.md).
