# Flatscreens

![A thinner display.](oredict:opencomputers:frontflatscreen1)

Flatscreens are thin versions of the standard [screen](screen1.md). They mount flush against a block face instead of occupying a full cube, but otherwise use the same `screen` component, [graphics cards](../item/graphicsCard1.md), tiers, resolutions, color depths, touch input, keyboards, and multi-block behavior as standard screens.

Each tier has a front and a back variant. The two variants put the display on opposite faces of the thin block, allowing the screen to sit on whichever side of a wall, floor, or ceiling suits the build. Their crafting recipes are mirrored to indicate the display side.

Like standard screens, adjacent flatscreens must have compatible tiers, orientations, and dye colors to form a multi-block display. Use a dye on a flatscreen to control which neighboring screens it joins; the dye is not consumed, and the color is not retained when the block is broken.
