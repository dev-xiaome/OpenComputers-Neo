# Audio Cable

![Route a tape drive to speakers.](oredict:opencomputers:audio_cable)

Audio cables carry tape-drive playback to [speakers](speaker.md). Place cable blocks in a connected path between a [tape drive](tape_drive.md) and one or more speakers. When the tape drive plays, every reachable speaker becomes an audio output; if no speaker is reachable, the drive plays directly from itself.

Audio cables are separate from OpenComputers network cables. They do not expose components or transfer power/data.
