Rack KVM
========

The rack KVM provides a private virtual [screen](../block/screen1.md) and [keyboard](../block/keyboard.md) for each of the three other slots in its [rack](../block/rack.md). Each console connects directly to its server without connecting the servers' component networks to each other.

Sneak-activate the rack KVM with a [remote terminal](terminal.md) to bind it. A rack KVM supports one remote terminal; binding another invalidates the previous binding.

Use the numbered buttons at the top of the remote terminal, or Ctrl+1 through Ctrl+3, to select a server. The numbers correspond to the three rack slots other than the slot occupied by the rack KVM. The selected server is also shown on the rack KVM's front panel.
